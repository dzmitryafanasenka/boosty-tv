### Process lessons (from the specs and the session log)

- **The planning text was wrong about the platform in two of three stories.** Both were found only on the device, after implementation:
  - 8.2 assumed the Android chooser works. On the pinned Google TV, `MATCH_DEFAULT_ONLY` and the system chooser see only the framework stub. It took three probe builds.
  - 8.3 assumed internal storage can be pulled over adb. Release builds are not debuggable (`run-as` is refused). This one was caught at spec time by one adb command.

  Lesson: when a story depends on Android platform behaviour (intents, storage, permissions), probe it on the pinned device while writing the spec. 8.3 did that and avoided a loopback; 8.2 did not. (A6)
- **Implementer agents had no Android SDK.** Kotlin was first compiled by the coordinator. 8.2's first build failed because `video/*` inside a KDoc opened a nested comment. Lesson: the coordinator compiles native changes right after the implementer returns, before the review fan-out; the 8.2 and 8.3 specs carried the "no slash-star in Kotlin comments" hint after that.
- **A batched adb key run lost a ↓ after Menu in 8.1.** The OKs that followed stored the wrong quality and speed and moved the owner's resume point by about 12 s. It was restored with the owner's approval. The device rules gained "settle about 1 s after Menu" and "space single seeks" (in `docs/testing.md` and the wiki adb guide). 8.2 and 8.3 ran step by step with a screenshot before each OK. The owner's resume points did not move: «Осталось 73 мин» and «108 мин» stayed unchanged through every 8.2 and 8.3 run.
- **The review fan-out found the most important 8.3 defect.** It was not the redaction: it was blog slugs and post ids in http paths, which reveal viewing history. Lesson: personal-data review of diagnostics must cover identifiers in paths, not only secrets. This is now in the wiki decision «Скрытая диагностика».
