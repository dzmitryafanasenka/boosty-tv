### Process lessons (from the specs and the two session logs)

- **Live probes paid off again:** 6.1's probe showed that style ranges count from the paragraph start, which corrected the spec before UI work (`docs/testing.md` Story 6.1). Every story ran a counts-only probe and removed it before the commit.
- **Device runs found what host tests could not:**
  - 6.3: a narrow chip under a full-width paragraph was skipped by D-pad.
  - 6.5/6.6: the focus ring was clipped at the column's bottom.
  - 6.7: unmounting the focused control sent focus to the author; a focus request before layout scrolled the column to the top.
  - All were fixed. The 6.7 rules are now in `docs/focus-map.md` and the wiki.
- **A device check changed the owner's data:** the 6.3 timestamp-chip check played ≥ 10 s, so the progress rule rewrote the owner's progress on that video («Осталось 19 мин» → «108 мин», `docs/testing.md` Story 6.3). Epic 4 had the same lesson (the feed head changed under a sleep test). Rule (A7): a device check that plays the owner's content stays under the progress threshold, or the owner approves the write first.
- **Missing live samples:** no live multiple-choice poll, `smile` block, finished poll or reachable file chip. Those paths rest on synthetic fixtures and the demo. The specs said so up front, which kept the claims honest.
- **Owner decisions mid-story:** 6.3 timestamp links went from option a to option b on the same day. Recording the supersession in the spec and `testing.md` kept the trail clear.
