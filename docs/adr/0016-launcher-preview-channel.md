# ADR 0016: «Продолжить просмотр» as an Android TV app preview channel

- Status: accepted (Story 12.5; the Aero TV behaviour gate passed on the device 2026-11-03; the owner's remote check follows the release-like build)
- Date: 2026-11-03
- Relates to: `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/spec-12-5-continue-history-launcher-channel.md`, `docs/research/watch-next-and-launcher-channel-spike.md` (Story 12.4), [ADR 0004](0004-progress-ownership-and-resume.md), `docs/focus-map.md` «Launcher entry», `docs/feature-parity.md` IMP-04 (FR67)

## Context

The owner's launcher, Aero TV (`tv.aero.home`), shows an app's cards when its tile is focused. Story 12.4 showed on the pinned Chromecast with Google TV that Aero ignores Watch Next and shows an app **preview channel** (`TvContract.Channels.TYPE_PREVIEW`) with its `PreviewPrograms`. The Story 12.5 gate (device, 2026-11-03) added: Aero picks up a new or changed channel at once; it **ignores an empty channel** and keeps the old cards; it replaces them when the channel gets a neutral card; and after a channel is deleted it keeps its own copy. Owner decisions 2026-11-03: an empty history shows one neutral card «Откройте Boosty TV»; OK on a video card opens the Player at the resume point; Back from it goes to Главная.

## Decision

- **One channel, kept.** `LauncherChannelModule.kt` (platform `android.media.tv.TvContract`, API 26+; minSdk stays 24 and below 26 the port answers `unsupported`) finds our channel by `COLUMN_INTERNAL_PROVIDER_ID = "continue"` (the provider returns only this app's rows), creates it once and never deletes it. Every sync inserts all new programs, then deletes the old ones, so the channel is never empty in between; if an insert fails (null or an exception), the programs this sync inserted are deleted, the old ones stay and the sync fails. The channel row is updated only when its stored name or app link differ. No `requestChannelBrowsable` (a system dialog; Aero does not need it). No androidx `tvprovider` dependency.
- **Programs mirror the Continue view.** `src/app/launcherChannel.ts` publishes `continueEntries(store.entries())` (same order, at most `MAX_CONTINUE_ENTRIES`): the Continue card's title rule (`videoDisplayTitle`, else «Без названия»), the author as the short description, a poster that passes `persistablePreviewUrl` (no query, so never a CDN token), else the app banner. An empty view publishes the neutral card (banner, «Откройте Boosty TV»), never an empty channel.
- **Sync on change only.** A projection key (order, ids, titles, authors, posters) decides; positions are not in it, so periodic progress ticks never sync. Nothing is sent before the store has read its disk. Syncs run one at a time and only the latest wanted list follows a running one. A failure is logged (outcome and count only) and does not count as published: the next change, or the next return to the foreground, sends again.
- **Account isolation.** `launcherChannelAccountStore` is registered with AccountScope after `progressAccountStore`; the wipe (sign-out, account switch) publishes the neutral card and waits for it. Each sync carries the AccountScope generation at the time it was wanted and is dropped when it changed, so an old account's list queued before the wipe is never sent after it. A failed neutral sync is logged and does not fail the wipe: the session is already gone; the next return to the foreground sends the neutral card again.
- **Permission.** `com.android.providers.tv.permission.WRITE_EPG_DATA` (normal: granted at install, no prompt; without it the insert throws `SecurityException`, Story 12.4).
- **External entry.** The card and channel intents are explicit-component intents to `MainActivity` with our action `io.github.dzmitryafanasenka.boostytv.action.OPEN_FROM_LAUNCHER` and string extras (`target`, and `blogUrl` / `postId` / `mediaId` for a video). No new intent-filter, no VIEW filter, no URL scheme. `MainActivity` hands the launch intent (`onCreate` without saved state) and `onNewIntent` (after `setIntent`) to the module, which keeps one pending open in memory; any other launch, or a launch from recents, drops it. JS takes it once (`takePendingOpen`) when the navigator is ready (cold start) and on every return to the foreground (warm start).
- **Outside input is checked.** The activity is exported (it is the launcher activity), so any app can send that explicit intent. JS opens the Player only when signed in and when the ref passes the progress ref schema **and** matches an entry of the current local Continue view; anything else opens Главная (signed out: the sign-in stack stays, Welcome on a cold start). The Player opens as from a Continue card: `[Shell, Player]`, origin Главная, focus id `home/continue/<mediaId>`, the resume point; locked or removed posts take the Player's existing unavailable path.
- **Demo and phases.** The demo never writes the channel and drops a launcher card; the channel is off without the player phase or the native module.
- **No logging** of titles, ids or intent URIs, natively or in JS; rejections carry the exception's class name only.

## Consequences

- The intent URIs in the provider carry the blog name and the post and media ids of each card (needed for the deep link); this is the content the card shows, not a secret. No signed URL, token or account id is written.
- The channel is a second, device-wide view of the account's Continue history for as long as the account is signed in; it is replaced by the neutral card on sign-out.
- Aero keeps its own copy of a channel; a card it still shows after a change (or the 12.4 probe's synthetic card) opens the app, and a stale ref falls back to Главная.
- The Google TV stock launcher on the pinned device runs in apps-only mode and shows no channels (Story 12.4); certification for its own row is not a goal.
- **Rollback:** a rollback build must first replace our programs with the neutral card (or delete them) once on start, before it stops syncing: just dropping `launcherChannel.start()` and the account store would leave the last account's cards in the provider and in Aero's copy (also after a later sign-out). Our channel row itself may stay; the TV provider is expected to drop an uninstalled app's rows (not verified on the device).
